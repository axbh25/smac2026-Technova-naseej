## Learning Rules

1. The saved card contains exactly three steps.
2. Each step has one large checkbox target.
3. Step progress is saved locally after every change.
4. Teach-back text accepts 10–400 characters.
5. Completion requires all three steps and a valid teach-back response.
6. The completed timestamp is stored locally.
7. A changed SkillCard invalidates its old learning progress.

## Day 9 Data Boundary

Day 9 sends no data to Firebase AI Logic.

The following remains local:

- Completed step numbers
- Teach-back response
- Completion timestamp
- SkillCard fingerprint

## Accessibility

- Minimum 48 × 48 touch targets
- Entire step card is tappable
- Icons are paired with text
- Progress is shown in text and visually
- Arabic uses RTL
- Screen remains vertically scrollable
