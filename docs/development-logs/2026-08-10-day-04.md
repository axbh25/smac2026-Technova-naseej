## Data Stored

One draft containing:

- Teacher nickname
- Teacher role
- Learner nickname
- Learner role
- Skill category
- Typed explanation

No account, location, photo, audio recording, or cloud data is stored.

## Design Decisions

1. The current local profile is the teacher.
2. The learner is stored inside the draft rather than as a second full account.
3. Only one draft is stored in the MVP.
4. The draft remains fully offline.
5. Microphone and camera are deferred.
6. AI does not process the draft yet.
7. SharedPreferencesAsync is acceptable for this one small non-critical draft.
