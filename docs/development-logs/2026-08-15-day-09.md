## Design Decisions

1. A saved SkillCard was required before learning could start.
2. Progress was bound to the exact card content, not only its draft.
3. Each checkbox change was saved locally.
4. Teach-back required at least 10 characters.
5. Completion required all three steps.
6. Completion required a valid teach-back response.
7. Editing a draft cleared the old card and progress.
8. Regenerating the card cleared old progress.
9. The feature worked in Airplane Mode.
10. No new package or cloud service was added.
