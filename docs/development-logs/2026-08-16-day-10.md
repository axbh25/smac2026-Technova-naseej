- Record audio

The device's installed Android text-to-speech service handled playback.

## Design Decisions

1. Speech remained optional.
2. One step played at a time.
3. Card language selected speech language.
4. Interface language did not alter lesson content.
5. Stop remained visible while speaking.
6. Replay appeared after completion.
7. Speech failure remained non-blocking.
8. Leaving the screen stopped speech.
9. Speech did not change learning progress.
10. No custom voice settings were added in the MVP.
